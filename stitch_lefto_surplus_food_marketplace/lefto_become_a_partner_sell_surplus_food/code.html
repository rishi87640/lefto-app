Merchant Helpdesk

Questions businesses usually ask

Everything you need to know about setting up your kitchen on LEFTO.

What is a Rescue Bag? expand_more
A Rescue Bag is a surprise package of perfectly good, freshly prepared unsold food from your day’s operation. Because surplus varies daily, you do not need to itemize individual dishes—just specify whether it is vegetarian or non-vegetarian and the broad category.
Can I choose what goes inside? expand_more
Yes, entirely. You pack whatever surplus food is fresh and safe at closing. You maintain complete culinary control over your brand's quality.
How do I set my price? expand_more
You set the price yourself. Most partners list Rescue Bags at 50% to 70% off their normal retail value to ensure rapid reservation by nearby conscious diners.
When do customers pick up? expand_more
You specify the exact pickup window that works with your daily schedule—typically the last 30 to 60 minutes before closing or right after lunch service.
Can I pause my listings? expand_more
Yes, at any moment. If your kitchen sells out completely during regular dining, simply toggle off your listing in the merchant app with a single tap.
What happens if nobody reserves a bag? expand_more
There are never any penalty fees or charges for unreserved bags. You only operate on success.
Is LEFTO available in my area (Greater Noida)? expand_more
Yes! LEFTO is actively onboarding food merchants across Alpha 1, Alpha 2, Gamma, Delta, Pari Chowk, and the Knowledge Park student hubs.
How are partner payments handled and what are the commercial terms? expand_more
Commercial terms depend on the applicable LEFTO partner agreement and will be shown transparently before onboarding is completed. Direct bank transfers occur weekly with full transaction reconciliation statements.
