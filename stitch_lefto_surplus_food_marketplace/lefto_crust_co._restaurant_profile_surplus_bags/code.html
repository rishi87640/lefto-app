About Crust & Co.

Crust & Co. is an artisanal pizzeria and Italian café established in 2021 in Greater Noida, celebrated for 48-hour slow-fermented sourdough pizza, authentic Napoli marinara, and wood-fired crusts crafted with imported flour and fresh local produce.

Store Policies & FAQs

Cancellation & Refund Window expand_more

You can cancel your reservation up to 2 hours prior to the start of the scheduled pickup window for a 100% wallet refund. Within 2 hours of pickup, cancellations are non-refundable to prevent kitchen preparation waste.

Allergen & Dietary Specifications expand_more

Pizzas and bakery items contain gluten, dairy (mozzarella, parmesan), and may contain traces of nuts. If you have severe allergies, please check in-person at the counter before collection or select bags marked specifically as 100% Vegetarian.

What if I arrive late for pickup? expand_more

Unclaimed bags after the 30-minute window will either be provided to local shelter staff or discarded per hygiene norms. We strongly recommend planning your arrival with 10 minutes buffer.
